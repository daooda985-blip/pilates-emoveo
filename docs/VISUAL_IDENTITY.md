# emoveo — visual language v1
## Creative premise
An elegant, restrained, slightly uncanny movement archive. The interface should feel like a page from a speculative field journal: part movement studio, part botanical / Japanese visual notebook, part quiet science-fiction document.
## Palette
- Ink Plum: #211A27 — navigation, deep panels, text on light surfaces.
- Night Aubergine: #38283C — atmospheric surfaces.
- Paper Mist: #E9E2DF — main canvas.
- Dust Rose: #B98C9E — soft accents and active states.
- Moon Lilac: #C5B5CB — secondary accents.
- Oxide Red: #A84F48 — rare punctuation, never large fills.
- Pearl: #F0DCE5 — text and highlights on dark surfaces.
## Typography
Use a high-contrast serif for editorial headlines and a clean sans-serif for controls, labels and technical information. Keep navigation uppercase and tracked, but maintain readable sizes.
## Graphic vocabulary
Broken circles / enso, petal geometry, orbital paths, breath lines, small vermilion marks, subtle grain, botanical silhouettes. Avoid generic lotus clip-art, gradients on every surface, excessive rounded cards and decorative kanji without meaning.
## Mascot: Mori
Mori is an abstract seed-spirit / folded petal, not a human or animal. It appears as a quiet observer and guide. Use sparingly: welcome states, empty states, short hints, loading / transition moments. No speech bubbles by default. Avoid infantilizing proportions or cartoon expressions.
## Symbol semantics
呼吸 (breath) may label breathing practices; 身 (body) may label body awareness. Use only where the context genuinely matches. Do not use decorative random characters.
## Responsive behavior
Desktop: editorial wide composition, asymmetry, generous negative space, horizontal rules.
Mobile: preserve the art direction but stack content, shorten decorative details, prioritize touch targets and clear labels.
## Accessibility
Maintain readable contrast, visible focus indicators, semantic labels and reduced-motion compatibility. Art assets are decorative unless they convey meaning.
