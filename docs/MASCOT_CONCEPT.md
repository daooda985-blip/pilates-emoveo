# emoveo — mascot concept and behavior (working brief)

## Status
- The current visual concept image is approved as the **working visual direction** for the mascot. It is not yet a final production model or a complete character sheet.
- **Mia (Миа)** is the current working name. It is provisional and may change.
- Character design, exact anatomy, proportions, animation rig and final asset specifications remain open.
- The mascot's broader world and cultural foundations are documented in [WORLD_MIA.md](WORLD_MIA.md).

## Character concept
Mia is a curious, kind and slightly unusual being. Her form is a hybrid of a creature, a plant and abstract energy. The visual direction uses an ethereal natural-fantasy atmosphere and a palette around plum, lavender, dusty rose, soft cream and luminous accents.

## Confirmed personality and interests
- Curious, kind and a little strange.
- Loves nature and feels connected to all four elements: water, earth, air and fire.
- Enjoys collecting unusual stones and herbs.
- Meditates, explores, rests and sings.
- Has her own ongoing life, habits and interests; she is not simply waiting for the user to open the app.

## Confirmed product behavior

### 1. Supportive relationship with daily activity
- Mia is connected to the user's daily activity and Pilates practice.
- She should encourage the user to return to practice.
- She must not shame, punish, guilt-trip or scold the user for missed days.
- A break in activity should not be framed as failure or loss.

### 2. Practice together
- During a workout, Mia trains alongside the user.
- She mirrors the movements in the generated Pilates routine.
- Her movement should correspond to the actual exercise sequence, not be a generic looping animation.
- Completing a full workout can contribute to visible changes in her body/form.

### 3. Development and personalization
- The direction combines automatic development and user customization.
- Automatic development may affect form, movement abilities and emotional expression over time.
- The user can personalize appearance, clothing and accessories.
- Exact stages, thresholds, attributes and transformations remain undecided.

### 4. Gifts and streaks
- A streak of 3 or more consecutive active days can unlock gifts for Mia, such as new appearance options.
- Gifts should feel like positive surprises, never compensation for punishment.
- Exact streak rules, reset behavior and reward catalogue remain undecided.

## Experience principles
- Encouragement without pressure; progress without shame.
- Mia accompanies the user; she does not judge the user.
- Evolution communicates practice, curiosity and care, not body ideals or weight-loss goals.
- The mascot should remain optional and must not obstruct the workout interface.

## Gradual design process
1. Character/world concept — in progress.
2. Name — working name Mia; final name not confirmed.
3. Visual identity — current concept approved as direction; production design still to be developed.
4. Expressions and movement — to be designed.
5. Progression and rewards — to be specified.
6. Implementation — only after behavior and assets are defined.

## Implementation note
This document records product direction, not completed functionality. Do not claim that mascot animation, exercise mirroring, progression, streak tracking, rewards, persistent world simulation or music generation are implemented. The older `assets/mori-mascot.svg` is a provisional experiment and is not approved artwork.
