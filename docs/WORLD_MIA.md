# Mia's world — living-world and culture brief

> Status: evolving creative canon. Confirmed user decisions are marked as established; proposals and unresolved lore are marked as provisional.

## 1. Mia — current character direction

### Established
- Working name: **Mia (Миа)**. The name is provisional, not final.
- The approved concept image is the current visual reference direction, not a finished production model.
- Character form: a hybrid of a creature, a plant and abstract energy.
- Character: curious, slightly strange and kind.
- Mia has her own ongoing life, habits and interests.
- She loves nature, collecting different stones and herbs, meditating and singing.
- She is connected to the energy of all four elements: water, earth, air and fire.

### Still open
- Final name, species name, anatomy, proportions, exact visual features, voice design and production model.
- Her precise origin and place in the world's history.

## 2. Home location

### Established
Mia has a distinct natural home location: a waterfall, with Mia meditating in lotus pose on a nearby stone.

### Working environmental direction
- Waterfall and a clear pool or stream.
- Moss-covered stones and small wet rocks.
- Herbs, flowers, ferns and unusual plants.
- Forest atmosphere, mist and subtle luminous particles.
- The location should feel alive, not like a static decorative background.

The waterfall is Mia's home place, but the wider setting is a whole living world, not a single isolated sanctuary.

## 3. The wider world

### Established
- The world is a **whole living world**.
- It exists independently of the user and is not merely a decorative room inside the app.
- Nature, places and inhabitants belong to a connected ecosystem.
- The world is connected to four elements: water, earth, air and fire.

### Provisional geography proposals
These are possible regions, not approved map canon:
1. Valley of Waterfalls — Mia's home.
2. Ancient Forest — unusual plants, trees and small creatures.
3. Sky Islands — clouds, wind and open air.
4. Stone Mountains — rocks, minerals and caves.
5. Flowering Plains — herbs, flowers and open natural spaces.

## 4. Mia's people / kind

### Established
- Mia is not the only being of her kind.
- Other beings share a recognizable common nature or lineage, but none is completely identical to Mia.
- The analogy is a people or species with shared traits and individual differences.
- Their forms may combine organic, botanical and energetic features.

### Society
- Their society is based on **harmony**, not a conventional ruling hierarchy.
- They are connected through a shared natural energy.
- They retain individual identity and freedom.
- Their relationships combine a sense of collective connection with personal friendship, attachment and independent choices.

### Life cycle
- Each being has an **individual life cycle**.
- Their development, pace of change and transformations can differ.
- No universal fixed sequence of life stages has been approved.

### Culture — working proposals
Possible cultural practices include:
- Collecting meaningful natural objects and remembering the stories of their discovery.
- Singing together or creating sound in natural spaces.
- Quiet periods for meditation and observation.
- Caring for plants, habitats and other beings.
These are creative directions to develop, not yet a fully finalized list of customs.

## 5. Four elements

The four elements are a foundational motif:
- **Water:** flow, change and adaptability.
- **Earth:** grounding, stability and growth.
- **Air:** breath, freedom and movement.
- **Fire:** warmth, vitality and transformation.

Mia is connected to all four. Do not assign her exclusively to one element unless the user later decides so.

## 6. Natural resonance — provisional worldbuilding concept

A possible way to express the connection among beings and nature is **natural resonance**. It is not constant mind-reading or compulsory telepathy. It may be sensed as subtle changes in sound, light, warmth, vibration or the surrounding environment.

This concept is a proposal for the mythology, not yet a confirmed supernatural rule.

## 7. Music — established creative direction

Music is a central part of Mia and the emoveo experience.

### Mia's own singing
- Mia loves to sing.
- Her voice is imagined as vibrational, delicate and otherworldly, beyond ordinary human hearing in the mythology.
- Its audible artistic interpretation should use gentle sustained notes, layered harmonies, natural resonance and a Nordic / Scandinavian folk-inspired atmosphere.
- The aim is an original musical identity, not copying existing traditional songs.

### Music library
- Mia also interacts with the emoveo music library.
- The intended direction combines her own singing with selected library compositions.
- She can act as a musical guide, selecting or shaping the sound atmosphere for Pilates, yoga, meditation, stretching and breathing practices.

### Product and safety distinction
The inaudible-frequency aspect is fictional worldbuilding. In the actual app, sound must be audible and comfortable, with appropriate volume controls. Do not make unsupported therapeutic claims about frequencies or vibrations.

## 8. Mia's ongoing life

Mia should continue to have her own habits and activities when the user is not present. Possible scenes:
- Meditating near the waterfall.
- Collecting stones or herbs.
- Exploring plants and unfamiliar places.
- Singing.
- Resting or sleeping.
- Interacting with other beings.
- Occasionally noticing and greeting the user.

Her life should not create pressure, guilt or a requirement to check in daily.

## 9. Origin and mythology — not decided

The following questions remain open:
- Did the people emerge from the combined energy of the four elements, a world tree, cosmic energy, natural evolution, or a combination?
- Do they know their own history, or is their origin partly mysterious?
- Is the world ancient, young, or renewed through cycles?

Do not treat any of these possibilities as established until the user chooses.

## 10. Product implementation status

This document records creative direction only. It does not mean the following features already exist:
- A rendered interactive world or explorable map.
- Autonomous simulation of Mia's daily life.
- Other animated inhabitants.
- Persistent collections of stones and herbs.
- Dynamic weather, seasons or world-state changes.
- Mia's singing voice, procedural music mixing or a connected music library.
- Natural-resonance effects.

These require separate design, assets, audio, engineering and product decisions.
